Buyer playbook

  1. Validate before volume. Order at MOQ, test with paid ads or a marketplace listing, and only scale SKUs with proven sell-through.
  2. Certify early. Lab testing (CE/FCC/EN71) takes 2–6 weeks. Start it with your sample order, not your bulk PO.
  3. Respect seasonality windows. Summer SKUs are bought by February; Q4 gifting SKUs by August. The calendar on each card is the buy signal.
  4. Own a backend. The best picks here have consumables — filters, brush heads, paper rolls. That's where durable margin lives.
  5. Differentiate or don't enter high-saturation tracks. Red "high saturation" badges mean you need a real angle: bundle, spec upgrade, or brand.
